## Casual Leave
Employees can take 12 days of casual leave per calendar year.
Casual leave should be requested through the employee portal.
Manager approval is required before leave is taken.

## Sick Leave
Employees can take 10 days of sick leave per calendar year.
A medical certificate may be requested for sick leave
lasting more than three consecutive working days.

## Working Hours
The normal working schedule is 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM,
Monday through Friday.
Employees have a one-hour lunch break.

## Work From Home
Eligible employees can work from home up to two days per week.
Manager approval is required.
Some roles may require employees to work from the office.

## Salary Payment
Salary is paid on the last working day of each month.
If the payment date falls on a bank holiday, the company
may process payment on the previous working day.

## Annual Leave
Eligible employees receive 15 days of annual leave per year.
Annual leave requests should be submitted at least seven days
before the planned leave, except in emergencies.

## Noisy Information
The company has a blue logo.
The office walls were painted white.
The cafeteria serves tea and coffee.
